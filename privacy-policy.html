Privacy Policy

NUMIA  ·  Last updated: May 24, 2026

Effective for all versions of NUMIA

NUMIA ("the App") is an independent math puzzle game developed and operated by the NUMIA team ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. For any privacy-related inquiries, you can reach us at support.numia@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. All game progress (your current level, scores, and settings) is stored locally on your device only using the device's built-in storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us under any circumstances. We have no servers, no accounts, and no backend that receives your data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, OS version, and language settings
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, view duration)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, in accordance with their policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (e.g. removing banner ads, hint packs). Note: the "Remove Ads" purchase removes only banner ads. Optional rewarded ads (used to earn hints) remain available by user choice. All purchases are processed exclusively by Apple (App Store). We do not receive, store, or have access to any payment information, credit card details, or billing data. Purchase transactions are governed by Apple's privacy policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the App will ask for your permission before allowing AdMob to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

5. Opt-Out of Personalised Ads

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable for NUMIA
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information at all, no such data exists on our end. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

7. Your Rights — EU/EEA Users (GDPR)

Since we do not collect or process any personal data ourselves, the majority of GDPR data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) do not apply to us directly — there is simply no data we hold about you.

For data collected by Google AdMob on your device, you can exercise your GDPR rights directly with Google:

  • Google Data & Privacy
  • Submit a data request to Google

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (DPA).

8. Your Rights — California Users (CCPA)

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to any third party — because we do not collect personal information in the first place. California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale of personal information. Since we hold no personal data, these rights are satisfied by default.

For advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's CCPA page:
https://policies.google.com/privacy/google-partners

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App. We retain no data on any external server — there is nothing to delete on our end.

10. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – AdMob Privacy & Terms
  • Apple In-App Purchase – Apple Privacy Policy

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
support.numia@gmail.com
