Privacy Policy

NUMIA  ·  Last updated: July 30, 2026

Effective for all versions of NUMIA

NUMIA ("the App") is an independent math puzzle game developed and operated by an independent developer ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. For any privacy-related inquiries, you can reach us at support.numia@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. All game progress (your current level, scores, and settings) is stored locally on your device only using the device's built-in storage (AsyncStorage). We have no servers, no accounts, and no backend that receives your data, and this data is not accessible to us under any circumstances.

The only exception is the optional leaderboard: if you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your total score is submitted to that service so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted.

2. Advertising (Yandex Advertising Network)

The App displays ads provided by the Yandex Advertising Network. We do not use Google AdMob or any Google advertising service.

Ads in NUMIA are non-personalised. We instruct the Yandex SDK not to collect personal data for ad targeting, for every user in every country. Your activity is not tracked across other companies' apps and websites, no advertising profile is built about you, and no advertising identifier is used to target ads to you.

To deliver and measure an ad at all, Yandex may still process limited technical information such as:

  • Device model, operating system version, and language settings
  • IP address (used for coarse regional ad delivery and fraud prevention)
  • Whether an ad was displayed, clicked, or completed

This data is processed by Yandex, not by us, in accordance with their policy:
https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

Ad formats are limited to a banner and an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint. The App shows no interstitial, app-open, or full-screen ads that interrupt play.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. Because ads are non-personalised and we do not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), there is no tracking to request permission for. You will not see a tracking prompt in NUMIA.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (hint packs and a "Remove Ads" option). The "Remove Ads" purchase removes the banner only — the optional rewarded video remains available if you choose to watch one for a hint. All purchases are processed exclusively by the platform's app store — Apple (App Store) on iOS and Google Play on Android. We do not receive, store, or have access to any payment information, credit card details, or billing data. Purchase transactions are governed by the respective store's privacy policy:
Apple Privacy Policy  ·  Google Privacy Policy

5. Additional Ad Controls

Ads are already non-personalised for everyone, so no opt-out is required. If you would like to further limit advertising data on your device, you can:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information at all, no such data exists on our end. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

7. Your Rights — EU/EEA Users (GDPR)

Since we do not collect or process any personal data ourselves, the majority of GDPR data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) do not apply to us directly — there is simply no data we hold about you.

No consent dialog is shown in the App because no personal data is collected for advertising purposes: ads are served in non-personalised mode for all users, including those in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland.

For data processed by Yandex on your device, you can exercise your rights directly with Yandex:
Yandex Privacy Policy

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (DPA).

8. Your Rights — U.S. State Privacy Laws (CCPA/CPRA & others)

NUMIA does not operate servers and does not collect personal information. Because ads are non-personalised and no advertising identifier is used for targeting, we do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California's CCPA/CPRA or comparable U.S. state privacy laws.

Residents of California and other U.S. states retain the right to know, delete, opt out, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may also use your device's advertising settings described in Section 5.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App. We retain no data on any external server — there is nothing to delete on our end.

10. Notifications

With your permission, the App may send local reminder notifications (for example, your daily challenge). These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — no data is sent to us or to any server, and we operate no push-notification service. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Alerts in the App, or in your device's system settings.

11. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Yandex Advertising Network – Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Apple In-App Purchase (iOS) – Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing (Android) – Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Game Center (iOS, optional leaderboard) – Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games (Android, optional leaderboard) – Google Privacy Policy
